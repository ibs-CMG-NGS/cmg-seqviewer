# Planning Archive

> **Archived plans** — completed or superseded documents kept for historical
> reference. Current plans are listed in [Planning Documentation](../README.md).

## Policy

A planning document is moved here when:

- all planned phases are implemented and released, or
- the plan was superseded by a newer implementation/design document.

Archived documents are not updated for later changes. Historical statements
such as "미구현" or "계획" reflect the state at the time of writing.
